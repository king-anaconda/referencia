5 Google Sign-In & Third-Party Services

The Service offers an optional Google Sign-In feature. If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from Google. This information is used solely to identify you within the Service.

We use Firebase Authentication (a Google service) to handle the login process. Firebase may process data on servers located outside India. By using Google Sign-In, you also agree to Google's Privacy Policy.

The Service also uses the Pinterest API to fetch public reference images. We do not share any personal user data with Pinterest. Images and pinner usernames displayed in the Service are publicly available on Pinterest and are subject to Pinterest's Privacy Policy.

6 Cookies & Tracking

The Service itself does not set any first-party cookies. However, the following third-party services may set cookies or similar technologies:

  • Firebase Authentication: Sets session cookies to keep you logged in.
  • GitHub Pages: May set technical cookies for hosting and security purposes.

We do not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect the functionality of Google Sign-In.

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy.

Upon your request, we will delete your authentication data within 30 days.
